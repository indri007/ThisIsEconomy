An important empirical finding concerns the communicative position of the artificial intelligence account. In the observed network, however, @grok recorded an Out-Degree of 42, while @prabowo recorded an Out-Degree of zero ($C_{\text{out}} = 0$). This difference indicates an asymmetry in observed outbound interaction involving institutional and AI-mediated accounts.
